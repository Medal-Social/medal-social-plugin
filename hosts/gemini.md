# Gemini Apps

In the Gemini web app, open Settings > Connected Apps > Add a custom app. Enter:

`https://mcp.medalsocial.com/plugins/marketing/mcp`

Complete OAuth in Medal, select the intended workspace and review the actual consent. Use `@` to select the connected app in your prompt. Ask for an unsent draft and open the returned Medal builder link.

As checked on 2026-09-21, Google's documentation restricts custom apps to adults in the US, personal Google accounts, English, and Keep Activity enabled. Availability may change. A work/school account or an account without the custom-app control cannot complete this route. Do not change location, account type or privacy settings merely to bypass the restriction.

This is a custom connection guide, not a claim of Google directory publication or successful Gemini acceptance. Gemini CLI extensions are a separate product and are not a substitute for a verified Gemini Apps connection.

Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137?hl=en
