# Listing copy

Name: Medal Social

Tagline: An idea in chat. An on-brand email you can edit.

## Description

Turn a launch brief, content idea or sketch your assistant can view into an on-brand campaign draft or inactive transactional template in Medal Social.

Brand Center supplies your voice, logo, colors, typography and footer. Reuse approved image assets or import a public image you authorize. Build with native text, image, CTA and layout modules, then open the real draft in Medal's visual email builder. Refine individual blocks without losing the edits your team already made.

Email tools create drafts only: they do not send campaigns, add recipients or activate transactional triggers. Additional tools provide authorized social-post, analytics and customer context, including separate post scheduling/publishing capabilities subject to their permissions and confirmation workflow.

A Medal Social account, workspace membership, appropriate permissions and completed Brand Center email settings are required. Hosted service access depends on your Medal account and plan. The integration package is free.
